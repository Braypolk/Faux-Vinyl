# Reconstruction batch C

Completed exclusive ordered indices 31, 33, 34, 40 and 43. Five final files are listed by absolute path in report.json. All use built-in image_gen reference reconstruction followed by PhotoCraft standardization. No app code, manifests, approvals, original references, or other batches were edited.

Finals are 648×648 RGBA WebP with a centered 640px circular face, 4px transparent margin, and centered transparent apertures (16px vinyl; 80px CD). Each final was viewed individually after the last edit. validate.py records dimensional and alpha checks in validation.json; qa-contact-sheet.png shows all five on a checkerboard for QA only.

The selected faces remain the foreground blue Californication side one, red American Teen CD, Dreamland eight-pink-head picture disc, clear Woodstock side one, and Father Figure family-photo CD. Sleeve seams, retaining hubs, packaging and sleeve branding were removed. American Teen's first generation retained tray spokes, so it was rejected and regenerated with explicit smooth-hub instructions. The rejected PNG and both prompts are retained.

Original generated PNGs and exact per-asset prompts are preserved here, including PhotoCraft command records and the reproducible standardize.py script. Generated edge speckles were removed with slightly inset, measured ellipses centered on the hub. Smooth backing sampled from each existing hub/label restores the small generated aperture oversize before cutting the exact centered hole; Father Figure has a continuous thin silver rim restored with PhotoCraft.

Material limitations: fine credits, legal lines, exact tiny logos, and track timings are not reliably legible in several references and remain generative approximations. Californication label scale/type and Father Figure photo detail are reconstructed rather than exact original pixels. Woodstock's clear vinyl is rendered as frosted silver grooves. These limitations are also recorded per asset in report.json.
