# Broader vinyl research — singles B

Research date: 2026-11-11. Exclusive scope: **vinyl only**, the eleven album IDs in this report. Read `static/disc-review/catalogue.json`, including its `approved` list. All eleven had zero vinyl candidates there; each already had an approved CD image. No CD searching was performed. No app, approval site, inventory, curation, or manifest files were modified.

Deliverable: `broader-search-singles-b-candidates.json` contains **24 candidates across all 11 albums**. All 24 retained images were downloaded unchanged into `broader-search-singles-b-downloads/` and visually inspected. Classification: **7 full physical disc photos/scans, 8 product mockups, 7 physical packaging photos, 1 partial photo, 1 label close-up**. Maximum three options per album; one each for Californication and Curtain Call avoids redundant/inferior alternatives.

`status: Official` describes the **release**, not the website hosting its photograph. Ultimate Queen is an unofficial fan archive of documented official pressings. Product renders are explicitly `mockup`, even where they show complete discs. Physical photos with packaging are conservatively `packaging`; notes explain whether the foreground record is completely visible. No images were generated, cleaned, cropped, background-removed or otherwise edited. Contact sheets in the download folder are inspection aids, not candidate artwork.

## Useful findings by album

- **forest-hill-drive — J. Cole, 2014 Forest Hills Drive.** [Interscope's 10-year edition](https://interscope.com/products/2014-forest-hills-drive-vinyl-10-year-anniversary-edition) supplies a 1920px blue-vinyl mockup, but both records remain partly hidden by the alternate sleeve. [RecordsAlbums' original Columbia 88875 05698 1 listing](https://recordsalbums.com/rap-hip-hop-vinyl-records-albums-lps-sale/8660-j-cole-2014-forest-hills-drive-lp-vinyl-record-for-sale.html) supplies actual record and side-A label photos. The record photo has clipped edges, overlap, glare and perspective. The readable brick label is useful reference but lacks the complete disc. **Standalone full-disc gap remains.** Anniversary image is an alternate physical edition, not an exact original-edition match.

- **planet-her — Doja Cat, Planet Her (Deluxe).** [Official artist store](https://store.dojacat.com/products/planet-her-5th-anniversary) explicitly identifies translucent pale-blue 5th-anniversary vinyl and lists deluxe bonus tracks. Its 900px image is a sleeve-overlap render. [Turntable Lab](https://www.turntablelab.com/products/doja-cat-planet-her-vinyl-2lp) provides a better 1800px actual black-2LP package photograph; page identifies RCA 2022, UPC 194399456811. Both records overlap. **No unobscured standalone disc found.**

- **purpose — Justin Bieber, Purpose (Deluxe).** [Famous Rock Shop](https://famousrockshop.com.au/products/justin-bieber-purpose-lp-picture-disc-b0024509-01) has two approximately 1024px physical photographs of the RSD picture disc, catalogue B0024509-01: prayer-pose front and white-shirt reverse. Circular records are visible inside clear protective wallets; plastic glare, stickers and framing remain. [Def Jam's official LP page](https://defjamshop.com/products/justin-bieber-purpose-lp) supplies a black-2LP sleeve-overlap mockup. **Picture-disc photos are the strongest physical-disc alternatives; plain black standalone gap remains.** Physical edition track contents should not be assumed identical to Spotify Deluxe.

- **back-in-black — AC/DC, Back In Black.** [Your Vinyl Shop's 1980 Atlantic copy](https://yourvinylshop.com/products/ac-dc-back-in-black-1980-atlantic-vg-vg-13) has a complete 1200px black record photograph with Atlantic side-one label. Full outer perimeter is visible against its inner sleeve, with reflections and some perspective. [Official AC/DC store](https://store.acdc.com/products/back-in-black-blackwhite-blend-vinyl-lp) adds a 1500px 50th-anniversary black/white marbled product mockup with the sleeve obscuring part of the disc. **Full physical photo found.**

- **after-hours — The Weeknd, After Hours.** Official XO store supplies clean images of **two complete separate discs**, without sleeves: [collector 001 splatter edition](https://xo.store/collections/after-hours/products/after-hours-album-collector-s-vinyl-001-2lp) and [black 2LP](https://xo.store/collections/after-hours/products/after-hours-2lp). These are product renders on their existing transparent backgrounds; retained as mockups. [Republic Records' matching collector listing](https://www.republicrecords.com/products/after-hours-collectors-vinyl-001-2lp) independently corroborates the official collector edition. **Excellent complete-disc mockup options; physical-photograph gap remains.**

- **kids — Mac Miller, K.I.D.S.** [Rapbay's official-black-pressing listing](https://shop.rapbay.com/mac-miller-kids-10th-anniversary-vinyl-record/) supplies two 600px complete physical record photos: red and green centre labels, each on a plain inner sleeve. Mild glare/perspective remain, but both circumferences are complete. Page identifies UPC 192641494314 and official double-black 10th-anniversary configuration; its March 2026 stock date should not be treated as the original issue date. [Turntable Lab's Rostrum yellow edition](https://www.turntablelab.com/products/mac-miller-k-i-d-s-yellow-colored-vinyl-vinyl-lp) provides an additional physical package photo, with discs still partly inside paper sleeves. **Two complete physical-disc photos found.**

- **jesus-is-king — Kanye West, JESUS IS KING.** [Official Def Jam blue-1LP listing](https://defjamshop.com/products/kanye-west-jesus-is-king-1lp) provides a 1000px complete blue-record mockup on an existing transparent background. This is an actual vinyl product depiction with spindle hole and centre label, not the square cover image. [Turntable Lab](https://www.turntablelab.com/products/kanye-west-jesus-is-king-blue-vinyl-vinyl-lp) adds a 2400px actual transparent-blue record photograph, with full disc unobscured in front of its sleeve. Transparent material shows background through it; perspective remains. **Complete mockup and physical package-reference options found.**

- **the-game — Queen, The Game.** [Ultimate Queen's physical-edition gallery](https://www.ultimatequeen.co.uk/queen/gallery/albums-2/the-game.htm) links high-resolution complete-record images: **2000px grey-marble 2015 Studio Collection vinyl** and **2500px 2020 picture disc**. The fan-site source is unofficial; the releases are official. [Queen's own June 2020 announcement](https://www.queenonline.com/news/pre-order-the-game-40th-anniversary-vinyl-and-merch-qol-exclusives) confirms the 40th-anniversary picture disc and 1980-copy edition. [Loud & Clear's silver half-speed LP](https://shop.loud-clear.co.uk/products/queen-the-game-half-speed-remastered-silver-vinyl-lp) supplies a third complete physical photo, identified as Hollywood Records D002946201 / 0050087508524. **Three strong full-disc options; fan gallery is especially useful.**

- **californication — Red Hot Chili Peppers, Californication (Deluxe Edition).** [Turntable Lab's coloured-2LP gallery](https://www.turntablelab.com/products/red-hot-chili-peppers-californication-colored-vinyl-vinyl-2lp) contains an 1800px actual photograph of the 25th-anniversary red/ocean-blue set. The foreground blue record is completely visible; red record overlaps and sleeve/inserts remain behind. Photographed hype sticker verifies edition. The [former artist-store product URL](https://music.redhotchilipeppers.com/products/californication-2lp-red-ocean-blue-vinyl) returned 404. **Usable full-record-in-package photo; standalone gap remains.** This physical edition is the standard album, not the Spotify deluxe track configuration.

- **curtain-call — Eminem, Curtain Call: The Hits (Deluxe Edition).** [Official Interscope listing](https://interscope.com/products/eminem-curtain-call-vinyl) supplies the correct original Curtain Call product mockup, with black records partly behind the sleeve. Its four-side tracklist verifies original album identity. [Eminem's own discography page](https://shop.eminem.com/pages/eminem-discography) has essentially the same presentation, so no redundant candidate retained. **Standalone full-disc gap remains.** Search results frequently substituted **Curtain Call 2**; these were rejected. [RareVinyl](https://uk.rarevinyl.com/products/eminem-curtain-call-the-hits-ex-uk-2-lp-vinyl-record-double-602498878965-788055) and [Awesomesince84](https://www.awesomesince84.com.au/products/eminem-curtain-call-the-hits) additional downloads proved to be the back sleeve, not discs, and were excluded.

- **death-of-a-bachelor — Panic! At The Disco, Death of a Bachelor.** [Tony's Muziekhuis](https://www.tonysmuziekhuis.be/nl/products/panic-at-the-disco-death-of-a-bachelor-silver-vinyl-lp) has a complete approximately 600px silver side-A physical photo against its plain inner sleeve. Source HTML was downloaded and confirms vinyl LP, 26 March 2021, barcode 75678645563. [Turntable Lab](https://www.turntablelab.com/products/panic-at-the-disco-death-of-a-bachelor-colored-vinyl-vinyl-lp) has an 1800px package photograph with a complete silver record in front, matching UPC 075678645563. **Standalone and higher-resolution full-record-in-package options found.**

- Pinterest custom fan-design results, cover-only squares, album-back-sleeve squares, and wrong-album Curtain Call 2 results were excluded. No known unofficial pressing was retained.
