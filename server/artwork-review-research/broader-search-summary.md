# Broader disc-artwork search

Research date: 2026-11-11. Four parallel researchers searched separate album groups; the final pass checked additional Discogs galleries. Every selected candidate was downloaded, decoded and visually inspected. This batch updates the temporary approval board only.

Added **93 options across 35 albums**, covering **46 of 48 missing album/format pairs**. Quality labels: 39 full, 26 packaging, 8 label, 5 partial, 15 mockup. The 39 earlier approvals are preserved, and the eight albums already approved in both formats stay hidden. New finds is the default view.

Only the CDs for **Quinn XCII — The Story of Us** and **A Letter to My Younger Self** remain unverified. This is a search gap, not a claim that those CDs do not exist. Label-only, packaged, partial, mockup and unofficial-release options are marked individually. Some physical editions differ from digital Deluxe track configurations; candidate notes preserve those differences.

## Rebuild and provenance

`broader-review-candidates.json` contains normalized IDs, format, quality, source page, original URL and SHA-256. The unchanged originals and display thumbnails live in `static/disc-review/images/`; research originals also remain in each researcher’s downloads folder. `broader-review-overrides.json` corrects two tray/protector images to the packaging category after final visual review.

Run `python3 artwork-review-research/expand_review.py` to prepare the downloaded research candidates, then `python3 artwork-review-research/build.py` to rebuild the approval site. The main-app artwork manifests and card database were checked and remain unchanged.
