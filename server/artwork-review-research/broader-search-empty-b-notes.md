# Broader artwork search — empty group B

Research date: 2026-11-11. Scope: both vinyl and CD for `life-of-pablo`, `american-teen`, `dreamland`, `everybody`, `woodstock`, and `father-figure-725a6a` only.

## Result and method

23 visually inspected candidates cover all 12 album/format slots: 9 `full`, 9 `packaging`, 4 `mockup`, and 1 `label`. Candidates are ordered by album/format, with the preferred option first. The JSON contains actual image URLs, source pages, absolute downloaded-file paths, and individual caveats. All source images are original downloaded bytes; no cropping, background removal, generated artwork, or other image edits were performed. App code, approval pages, inventory, curation and manifests were not changed.

Read `static/disc-review/catalogue.json`: none of the six assigned albums has existing candidates or approved IDs. Album titles, artists and Spotify URIs matched the assigned scope. Did not re-scan MusicBrainz cover-art galleries or earlier cached artwork. Used web search/image search, official Shopify product JSON, physical retailer photographs, and newly fetched public Discogs release metadata/galleries. A few MusicBrainz *metadata* relations were read solely to identify Discogs release IDs; no MusicBrainz artwork was used.

Shopify `.json` responses and Discogs release/master responses are retained in the download folder as provenance. Product JSON was particularly useful because indexed search images often exposed only the cover while the same product gallery contained full discs. Initial default Python lacked SSL; `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3` with network escalation worked. Discogs image downloads initially returned 403 with default urllib headers; ordinary `User-Agent: Mozilla/5.0` and `Referer: https://www.discogs.com/` succeeded. The supplied signed image URLs are the exact API URLs, without altered size/signature paths.

`Official`/`Unofficial` describes the physical release, not ownership of the image or reuse rights. For Discogs-based official classifications, the catalogue/label/format and matching printed disc were checked; these are community release records, not direct label attestations.

## Kanye West — The Life of Pablo

- **Vinyl:** two full real photographs from [High-Fidelity LA's release gallery](https://highfidelityla.com/release/9074023/kanye-west-the-life-of-pablo): green marbled side C and amber/translucent side A. The retailer explicitly identifies the format as **Unofficial Release** and warns that Discogs-supplied images may differ from the item for sale. Its image filenames refer to Discogs release 9654970; its page path is the retailer's own listing ID. Do not treat its current release-year field as proof of the pictured pressing's year. Both selected discs are complete; the blue variant was rejected because its circumference touches/crops at the frame edges.
- **CD:** [PanthersCDs' Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1585072110/kanye-west-the-life-of-pablo-cd-2016) explicitly says custom made. Selected its real open-case collage-disc photo, with perspective and tray hub intact. The listing is unavailable, but the indexed original photograph downloads successfully. A second [eBay listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/336021997229) has an orange repeat-text disc in a case rendering/composite; clearly marked `mockup` and `Unofficial`.
- **Gap:** no official physical pressing established. No clean standalone CD found. [JWray's product](https://jwrayrecords.com/products/kanye-west-the-life-of-pablo-vinyl) confirms unofficial status but its sole image was cover-only, so excluded. [Clarity](https://clarityrecords.net/vinyl/kanye-west-the-life-of-pablo-2lp-coloured-vinyl-unofficial/) and [ValueYourMusic](https://www.valueyourmusic.com/items/394685929199-kanye-west-the-life-of-pablo-cd-unofficial-brand-new-2016-updated-mix-ye) also identify unofficial editions; no stronger disc asset was selected from them.

## Khalid — American Teen

- **Vinyl:** [Newbury Comics' exclusive pressing](https://www.newburycomics.com/products/khalid-american_teen_exclusive_2_lp) has separate full standalone disc photographs for sides ONE and THREE in its Shopify gallery, rather than just a sleeve mockup. Downloaded original 700 x 1000 images. Disc color looks pale mint green; the current page color field says Clear, so notes preserve that distinction. Correct red American Teen/RCA labels are visible.
- **CD:** [Discogs European release 9991009](https://www.discogs.com/release/9991009), 2017, RCA/Sony/Right Hand 88985414322, has a straight-on scan of the full red disc in a white tray. `packaging`, because the tray spindle and surrounding case remain. Downloaded and inspected image index 2.
- **Gap:** no independent standalone CD scan found. Checked US [10028595](https://www.discogs.com/release/10028595), Japan [12201800](https://www.discogs.com/release/12201800), and Korea [24828494](https://www.discogs.com/release/24828494); their usable images were fronts/backs/stickers rather than discs. [Musik-Sammler](https://www.musik-sammler.de/release/khalid-american-teen-cd-1561308/) exposes a five-image gallery but explicitly pixelates pictures for signed-out visitors; not used. [Joe's Albums](https://www.joesalbums.com/products/khalid-american-teen-vinyl) has large real black-vinyl photographs, but its sleeve masks part of the disc; retained raw downloads, omitted from shortlist because Newbury's complete discs are better.

## Glass Animals — Dreamland

- **Vinyl:** [RareVinyl's actual 2020 Zoetrope picture-disc listing](https://uk.rarevinyl.com/products/glass-animals-dreamland-uk-vinyl-picture-disc-lp-album-b0032101-01-874904) provides both sides at 1280px: pink heads on blue, and blue heads on purple. Both are physical photographs in clear PVC. The blue side is preferred because no sticker crosses its printed face; the purple side's upper-left is masked by a title sticker. Both remain `packaging`, with plastic glare/seams. Catalogue B0032101-01, barcode 602507152611.
- **CD:** [Discogs UK release 15734342](https://www.discogs.com/release/15734342) has a clean complete standalone silver disc scan with red handwriting, palm and car, image index 5. The sleeve edition is Amazon signed, but the CD itself is unsigned. The [official store's CD description](https://shopus.glassanimals.com/products/dreamland-cd) independently describes the hand-drawn disc art. Alternate: [official Real Life Edition CD](https://shop.glassanimals.com/products/dreamland-real-life-edition-cd), rear transparent-case rendering. This is the expanded 25-track 2022 edition; labeled `mockup` rather than a real photograph.
- **Gap:** no clean standalone vinyl disc found. [Official standard LP](https://shop.glassanimals.com/products/dreamland-vinyl-lp) only shows the disc partly behind the sleeve. Newly inspected blue-vinyl [15724792](https://www.discogs.com/release/15724792) has cover/back/sticker only; picture-disc [15733421](https://www.discogs.com/release/15733421) has complete-but-sleeved photographs inferior to RareVinyl's. US CD [15726402](https://www.discogs.com/release/15726402) only supplied cover/back; Real Life [24115781](https://www.discogs.com/release/24115781) did not improve on the selected sources.

## Logic — Everybody

- **Vinyl:** [Discogs 32756250](https://www.discogs.com/release/32756250) contains a complete isolated photograph of the US 2024 Amazon Music exclusive transparent pomegranate LP, image index 8. Blue side-A label, correct A mark and tracks. Label/catalogue: Def Jam/Visionary B0026672-01 JK02. The source contributor appears to have already isolated the photograph; this research did not edit it. Alternate: [Plaid Room's used black-vinyl gallery](https://www.plaidroomrecords.com/products/logic-27-everybody-10498879), side D beside the rear sleeve. Real photograph, full center label, partly hidden circumference: `packaging`.
- **CD:** [Canadian CD 35002757](https://www.discogs.com/release/35002757), 2017, B002667002, has a standalone full blue/red/white disc scan, image index 4. This was found among a 44-image gallery dominated by booklet pages. Alternate: [US limited numbered CD 10324373](https://www.discogs.com/release/10324373), open-case real photograph, complete CD face with hub: `packaging`.
- **Gap:** clean standalone **black** vinyl still missing. Original official black LP [10498878](https://www.discogs.com/release/10498878) only offered dark close-ups of labels on a turntable, with spindle obstruction; not shortlisted. US CD [10232804](https://www.discogs.com/release/10232804), Europe [13165243](https://www.discogs.com/release/13165243), Australia [12493876](https://www.discogs.com/release/12493876), and alternate Canada [34732545](https://www.discogs.com/release/34732545) yielded cover-only or front/back galleries. Top Copy's two-image gallery was sealed sleeve fronts/backs, excluded. Unofficial vinyl versions in the master were not used because official options were found.

## Portugal. The Man — Woodstock

- **Vinyl:** [Turntable Lab](https://www.turntablelab.com/products/portugal-the-man-woodstock-180g-vinyl-lp) has a 2400px real photograph of the entire black record lying before an open gatefold. Best black-vinyl option, but elliptical perspective and packaging remain. [2023 clear reissue 28515670](https://www.discogs.com/release/28515670) has a whole clear record inside a branded transparent inner sleeve, image index 5; sleeve lettering overlays the top of the transparent record and must not be mistaken for disc printing. Third fallback: [original black LP 10627901](https://www.discogs.com/release/10627901), clean side-ONE label scan, explicitly `label` because the record circumference is absent.
- **CD:** two full standalone black/white Atlantic disc images: [digipak 10792551](https://www.discogs.com/release/10792551), image index 3, and [jewel case 11451094](https://www.discogs.com/release/11451094), index 3. Both European 2017, catalogue 7567866083; full circumference and central hole visible.
- **Gap:** no straight-on standalone whole vinyl without packaging. Pink limited edition [10990457](https://www.discogs.com/release/10990457) supplied only label close-ups, not a full pink LP. [Antone's CD](https://antonesrecordshop.com/products/portugal-the-man-woodstock-cd), US [10436374](https://www.discogs.com/release/10436374), and Canada [10529546](https://www.discogs.com/release/10529546) were front/back-heavy; US [27376452](https://www.discogs.com/release/27376452) had a complete CD in a black tray, inferior to the two standalone scans. Warner's [official CD product](https://store.warnermusic.com.au/ms/products/woodstock-cd) verified the album/format but added no better disc visual.

## Jon Bellion — Father Figure

- **Vinyl:** [official Jon Bellion shop](https://shopjonbellion.com/products/father-figure-vinyl) has two separate full clear-vinyl images: record A/B with brown family-photo label, record C/D with wedding-photo label. Original 2400 x 1700 files explicitly named `JB_FF_VinylMock_VinylAB_Only.png` and `JB_FF_VinylMock_VinylCD_Only.png`. Both are **official promotional mockups**, not real pressing photographs; correctly tagged `mockup`.
- **CD:** [Discogs 33699312](https://www.discogs.com/release/33699312), US 2025 Beautiful Mind Records CD, image index 4, is a real open-case photograph with the entire printed disc face visible. Family image, Jon Bellion/Father Figure text, Beautiful Mind/Faultline marks; slight perspective, glare and hub remain. Exact retail barcode/pressing may differ from initial pre-order metadata (Discogs catalogue remains TBA).
- **Gap:** no full standalone real CD photograph and no verified real standalone vinyl photograph found. [Official CD store](https://shopjonbellion.com/products/father-figure-cd) only supplies a closed jewel-case front, deliberately excluded from candidate JSON. A [Reddit post about the CD](https://www.reddit.com/r/JonBellion/comments/1x5f3ub/father_figure_cd/) appeared in search, but the source-page fetch failed, so its image was not promoted. The single CD edition in newly fetched Discogs master 3946274 supplied the open-case option above.

## Integration notes

Keep the unofficial Kanye candidates separate in presentation. Do not silently promote `packaging`, `label` or `mockup` to `full`: a complete circle visible in a case is useful but not a standalone disc image. No square cover-only image was admitted. For strongest immediate full-disc options, use the two Newbury Khalid LPs, original Dreamland CD, pomegranate Everybody LP, Canadian Everybody CD, and either Woodstock CD. Father Figure vinyl is a strong official mockup fallback. Exact local paths and source URLs for every proposed asset are in `broader-search-empty-b-candidates.json`.
